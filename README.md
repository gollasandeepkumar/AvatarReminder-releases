# Avatar Reminder App

A free Mac menu bar app: an animated character walks onto your screen and asks whether you've had some water. Answer **YES** and it celebrates; choose **Remind me later** and it asks again soon. Routines for stretching, eye rest, posture and breathing, your own reminders, meetings, characters you can build yourself, and more.

**Website and live demo:** https://gollasandeepkumar.github.io/AvatarReminder-releases/

## Install

- [Download the latest DMG](https://github.com/gollasandeepkumar/AvatarReminder-releases/releases/latest), open it, and drag the app to Applications.
- Or with Homebrew: `brew install --cask gollasandeepkumar/tap/avatar-reminder`

The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time you open it macOS says it can't check it. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, click **Open Anyway** next to Avatar Reminder App, and confirm. After that, new versions arrive as updates.

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon and Intel. Free. Everything stays on your Mac.

This repository holds the downloads, the update feed (`appcast.xml`) and the website. The app's source is private.
