What it does

  • Routines. Water, stretch, rest your eyes, posture and breathing, plus your own reminders: every few minutes, at set times, or once.
  • Polite. It waits while you're away, on a call, in a Focus or watching something full screen, and shows a minute after.
  • Habits. Daily goals, streaks and a week's history in the menu bar.
  • Characters. Five built in and a gallery to choose from. Build your own from parts, or turn a drawing, photo, GIF or video into one.
  • Personality. Cheerful, Coach, Calm, Cheeky, Buddy or Mom, in English, Hinglish, हिन्दी, తెలుగు or தமிழ். It can read reminders aloud.
  • Plain words. Type "stretch every 50 minutes on workdays" and it sets it up.
  • Meetings and time limits. A heads-up before each meeting with a Join button, and a gentle nudge when an app or site's daily time runs out.
  • For developers. Scripts, AI agents (MCP), Shortcuts and a local webhook can speak through your character: avatar ask "Deploy?" --option Ship --option Wait.

Installing

  1. Download the DMG, open it, and drag Avatar Reminder App to Applications. Or install it with Homebrew (above).
  2. Open it. The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS says it can't check it. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Avatar Reminder App, and confirm. You only do this once.
  4. The water-drop icon appears in the menu bar, and a short setup guides you through the rest. New versions arrive as updates.

Private by design

No account, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your Mac: your routines, your history, your characters. The features that use Apple Intelligence run on the Mac too; only Image Playground's ChatGPT style, if you choose it to make a character, goes to OpenAI.
